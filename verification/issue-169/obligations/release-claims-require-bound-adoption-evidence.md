# release-claims-require-bound-adoption-evidence

## Why this obligation exists

PF-11 found that a policy implication and the fact of consumer adoption had been combined. Actual
adoption belongs to consumer R5 evidence. A separate release guard must prevent coordination links
from being reported as that evidence.

## Failure scenario and boundary

A checklist marks Basileus/Exarchos complete from open issue URLs or a local surrogate smoke without
exact consumer commits, package bytes, protected compatibility results, and release disposition.

## Assigned proof

- Rung: R3, deterministic validation of machine-readable release/adoption evidence.
- Missing artifact: policy-as-data evidence gate.
- Proposed kill: issue URLs only, mismatched consumer SHA, or semantic version without digest must all
  refuse completion; missing external records remain Indeterminate.
- Current disposition: Unproven.
